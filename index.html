IndiaUnbox

End-to-end sourcing from India — RFQ to doorstep
Open indiaunbox.com
indiaunbox.com

A buyer abroad describes a product. IndiaUnbox finds and verifies Indian manufacturers, then manages sampling, factory audits, production, inspection, compliance and delivery — anywhere from EXW to DDP.

The hard part is not the catalogue, it is trust and state. Every order moves through ten stages, each one something a buyer can check, and the whole pipeline has to stay legible to someone in London who cannot visit the factory.

SkilledMBA

An index of AI tools for the MBA community
Open skilledmba.com
skilledmba.com

There are more AI tools than anyone can evaluate. SkilledMBA curates them and maps each one to the MBA subjects, skills and roles it actually serves, so a finance student and an HR lead arrive at different, correct answers.

The work I am most pleased with is the taxonomy. The categories are built around how someone searches — by subject, by role, by skill — rather than how tools describe themselves. Routing and schema both follow from that, which is what keeps a thousand entries navigable instead of a long list.
