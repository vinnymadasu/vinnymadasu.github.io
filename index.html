IndiaUnbox
End-to-end sourcing from India — RFQ to doorstep

A buyer abroad describes a product. IndiaUnbox finds and verifies Indian manufacturers, then manages sampling, factory audits, production, inspection, compliance and delivery.

The hard part is not the catalogue, it is trust and state. Every order moves through ten stages a buyer can check, and the pipeline must stay legible to someone who cannot visit the factory.

  • PIPELINE10 tracked stages, brief to delivery
  • COVERAGE16 manufacturing clusters, 5 export ports
  • VERIFICATIONEvery supplier human-checked, NDA on every brief

SkilledMBA
An index of AI tools for the MBA community

There are more AI tools than anyone can evaluate. SkilledMBA curates them and maps each to the subjects, skills and roles it serves, so a finance student and an HR lead get different, correct answers.

The work I am most pleased with is the taxonomy — categories built around how people search rather than how tools describe themselves. Routing and schema follow from that.
